What is Ad-development?

Definition- Ad-development means how much we developed till now compared to other people.
To make it easier to understand, it is how much we developed(we means Ad-group) since January 14 2011, since Ad-vinak and Tim created ad-group version 1.0.
It is also defined as how many steps we are ahead of other people.
It can be positive or negative.
The current Ad-development is -50, since the Ad-members did not develop at all since 2023 December, When the Ad-group version 4.0 was created.
